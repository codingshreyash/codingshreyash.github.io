Scanning engine

Built-in OCR is free and offline — good on crisp restaurant receipts, shakier on long faded grocery tape. Add a vision-model key and /split will read the photo with an LLM instead: far better on abbreviations like ORG CHKN THGH. The key is stored only in this browser’s localStorage and is sent straight to the provider.

Check the lines

Tap the photo to enlarge it and compare. Fix any name or price that came out wrong.

The lines

Tap a name chip to take someone off an item, or use − / + to change how many ways it splits.

Tax, tip & fees

Split in proportion to what each person’s items came to — so nobody pays tax on a steak they didn’t eat.
